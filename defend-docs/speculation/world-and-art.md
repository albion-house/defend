# world and art sketches

none of this is promised.

## saltmarsh vigil

a brackish gate. reeds. low fog. field brass. warm path marks against cold water.

the current first playable already points in this direction.

## emberglass quarry

a fractured quarry with rails, hot glass, and short sightlines.

it might be a useful contrast test someday. for now it is only a sketch.

## future experiments

theme manifests, generated asset catalogs, atlas inspection, shader studies, and a larger design system may become useful later.

they are garden material until the playable game actually needs them.
