# expedition mode

Expedition is a campaign mode that alternates between two distinct experiences:

1. Fast-moving run-and-gun overworld combat.
2. Strategic stronghold assaults built around deploying the Core.

Players leave a safe Relay and push north through hostile territory, fighting waves of enemies, collecting resources, choosing Boons and Surges, and searching for the next enemy stronghold.

When a stronghold is discovered, the pace changes.

Players deploy the Core, establish a temporary foothold, build defenses, explore the facility, clear rooms, defeat a boss, and activate a Relay.

Activating a Relay expands the network, secures territory, and unlocks the next stage of the journey north.

The campaign rhythm is:

Relay
↓
Run-and-Gun Overworld
↓
Stronghold Discovery
↓
Deploy Core
↓
Build & Explore
↓
Boss Fight
↓
Activate Relay
↓
Push North
↓
Repeat

The overworld emphasizes action, momentum, and survival.

The strongholds emphasize exploration, teamwork, defense, and territory control.

Together they create a game that combines the arcade action of Ikari Warriors, the exploration of Zelda, the territory expansion of StarCraft, and the trap-and-defense gameplay of Orcs Must Die.

The ultimate goal is not simply to survive.

The goal is to push farther north, reactivate the Relay network, reclaim territory from hostile forces, and create memorable stories with friends along the way.

Expedition is not a tower defense campaign.

Expedition is a co-op action-adventure mode that alternates between fast arcade combat and strategic stronghold assaults.

Players push north through hostile territory, reactivate a network of Relays, establish temporary footholds, and slowly reclaim a dying world.

The goal is to create memorable stories, discoveries, close calls, and teamwork.
